Join the Team

We are actively recruiting for the Spring 2026 season. If you are interested in joining the team, please visit our recruitment page to learn more.

Where We Row

We row out of Bladensburg Waterfront Park, located on the Anacostia River in Bladensburg, Maryland. The park is around a 15 min drive from campus.

Indoor Training
Facility

When we can't go out on the water, we train at the team's Erg Room in Cole Field House. Training on the erg allows us to put in work off the water and stay in shape during the winter months.

Regattas

Each season, the team competes at several regattas. They are perfect opportunities for us to show off our hard work and represent the University. Check out our schedule for the season here.

Social

When we are not training or competing, we spend plenty of time together as a team. In addition to hanging out around campus, we host social events with other clubs, and a formal at the end of the year.
