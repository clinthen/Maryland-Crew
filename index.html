Join the Team

No prior rowing experience is required—over 85% of our roster walked on in college. Whether you're an experienced oarsman, coxswain, or brand-new athlete looking to compete at a high level, we'll teach you everything from day one.

On The Water

Where We Row

We row out of Bladensburg Waterfront Park, located on the Anacostia River in Bladensburg, Maryland. The boathouse is a 15-minute drive from campus, and the team organizes daily carpools for all athletes without cars.

Winter & Land Training

Indoor Training Facility

When we aren't on the water, we train right on campus inside the team's dedicated Erg Room in Cole Field House. Equipped with Concept2 ergometers and weights, it keeps our crews sharp throughout the winter months.

Competition

Regattas & Travel

Every semester we travel across the Mid-Atlantic and Northeast to race against top collegiate programs—culminating in spring 2,000-meter championships like Knecht Cup, MARC, and Dad Vail.

Why Row at Maryland?

Maryland Men's Crew is one of the premier student-run club sports at the University of Maryland. Most of our top varsity rowers never touched an oar before arriving in College Park—if you are competitive, coachable, and willing to work hard, you can earn a seat in a racing shell.

Novice Program

Dedicated coaching to teach technique, boat handling, and racing.

Coxswains Needed

Steer the boat, lead the crew, and call race strategy on the water.

Typical Practice Schedule

  • Monday – Friday (Water) 5:30 AM – 7:30 AM @ Bladensburg Waterfront Park
  • Afternoon / Winter Erg 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM @ Cole Field House Erg Room
  • Saturday Morning 7:00 AM – 9:30 AM @ Bladensburg Waterfront Park

* Morning practices wrap up in time for 8:00 AM classes on campus, and team carpools leave from campus dorms/apartments daily.

Everything You Need to Know

FAQ

Not at all! Over 85% of our team started rowing in college. Every fall and spring we recruit a full Novice squad and teach you everything from basic ergometer form to rowing in an eight-person racing shell.
Bladensburg Waterfront Park is only 15 minutes from campus. Upperclassmen and teammates with cars organize daily carpools from North Campus, South Campus, and nearby apartments (like Terrapin Row) so everyone has a guaranteed ride to and from practice.
We practice in the early mornings (around 5:30 AM – 7:30 AM) specifically so practice never conflicts with your academic classes, labs, or part-time jobs. Our roster includes Engineering, Computer Science, Business, and Pre-Med majors who maintain strong GPAs while competing.
The coxswain sits in the stern of the boat, steers the shell using the rudder, motivates the crew over a microphone system, and executes race strategy. Ideal coxswains are lighter in weight (typically 115–135 lbs), vocal, competitive, and sharp under pressure. No experience is needed—we train novice coxswains alongside our coaches.
Because we are a UMD Club Sport, semester dues help cover regatta entry fees, boat bay rentals at Bladensburg Waterfront Park, shell and launch maintenance, and travel/hotel costs for away regattas. We offer a free trial period at the start of the semester before dues are collected, as well as payment plans.
